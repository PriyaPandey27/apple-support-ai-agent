## 1. Overview & Current Status

The assignment requires a golden evaluation set of 150–250 hand-labelled examples.

### Current Dataset Status (Transparent Audit):
- `data/golden_eval_template.csv` contains **200 sampled conversations** extracted from the held-out `golden_eval` split.
- The columns `conversation_id`, `customer_message`, `apple_response_actual`, `intent_auto`, and `resolution_status_auto` are populated.
- The human ground truth columns (`human_intent_label`, `human_reply_quality_1to5`, `human_notes`) are currently blank.
- `data/human_validation_subset.csv` contains **50 calibration examples** that were provisionally reviewed during initial harness development.
- **Current Status:** 200 examples were sampled for the golden evaluation set; 50 were provisionally reviewed during initial calibration. The remaining examples require independent human annotation before this can be considered a fully hand-labelled golden set.

### Purpose of Hand-Labeling:
1. **Independent Intent Ground Truth:** Silver labels (`intent_auto`) were generated by regex rules. True evaluation requires independent human annotation to test whether the rules and ML models match human perception.
2. **LLM-as-a-Judge Calibration:** Providing human quality ratings (1–5) allows computing inter-rater agreement (Exact %, Within-1-pt %, Spearman $\rho$, Cohen's $\kappa$).
3. **Escalation Safety Auditing:** Verifying whether high-risk inquiries (security, billing, cracked glass) are correctly escalated.

## 2. How to Label the Examples

You can label the dataset in either of two ways:

### Method A: Using the Interactive Terminal Tool (Recommended)
Run:
```bash
python label_golden_set.py
```
This CLI tool steps through unlabeled rows, displays the customer tweet and Apple response, accepts 1-key inputs for intent (0–9) and quality (1–5), and saves continuously to `data/golden_eval_template.csv`. You can pause and resume (`q` to save and exit) at any time.

### Method B: Directly in Excel / VS Code / Google Sheets
Open `data/golden_eval_template.csv` and manually populate:
1. `human_intent_label`: Select from the 10 taxonomy categories below.
2. `human_reply_quality_1to5`: Assign an integer rating from 1 to 5.
3. `human_notes`: Optional notes (e.g. edge case reasoning, ambiguity).

## 3. Intent Taxonomy & Annotation Criteria

Assign one of the following 10 mutually exclusive intents:

| Intent Label | Core Definition | Positive Examples (Include) | Negative Examples (Exclude) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `autocorrect_text_bug` | Complaints about the iOS 11.1 keyboard defect converting the letter 'I' into symbols (`[?]`, `A ?`, `i[?]`). | *"Why does typing I turn into an exclamation box?"*, *"Fix the i autocorrect glitch"* | General typing lag or autocorrect dictionary errors without the letter 'i' bug. |
| `account_security_icloud` | Apple ID, iCloud account lockout, passwords, two-factor authentication (2FA), account hacks. | *"Locked out of my Apple ID"*, *"Can't receive verification code on trusted number"*, *"Password reset failed"* | Billing or subscription charges associated with an Apple ID. |
| `billing_repair_order` | Invoices, refunds, unauthorized charges, warranty claims, repair status, Genius Bar bookings. | *"Charged twice for Apple Music"*, *"Where is my replacement iPhone?"*, *"Need appointment to repair phone"* | Physical cracked screen inquiries that don't mention billing/warranty. |
| `battery_power` | Excessive battery drain, device shutting down, charging cable issues, battery health degradation. | *"Battery dies in 2 hours after updating"*, *"iPhone won't charge with cable"*, *"Phone turns off at 30%"* | General unresponsiveness where battery is not explicitly mentioned. |
| `screen_hardware_damage` | Physical damage to glass, display distortion, non-responsive touch screen, Touch ID/Face ID failure. | *"Screen cracked after drop"*, *"Touch screen not responding to swipe"*, *"Face ID disabled"* | Pure software UI cosmetic glitches where hardware is intact. |
| `connectivity` | Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular connection, hotspot, carrier signal dropping. | *"Can't connect to home Wi-Fi"*, *"Bluetooth disconnects from car"*, *"No service after iOS update"* | App-specific network failure (e.g. only Spotify won't stream). |
| `ios_software_update` | Issues installing, downloading, or functioning immediately after an iOS or macOS update. | *"iOS 11.0.3 update stuck on installing"*, *"Update slowed down my entire iPhone 6s"*, *"Can't restore update"* | Specific isolated issues with battery or autocorrect caused by an update (classify under the specific defect). |
| `app_appstore_media` | App Store downloads, iTunes, Apple Music library sync, Podcasts app crashing. | *"Can't download apps from App Store"*, *"Apple Music songs disappeared"*, *"Podcast app crashes on launch"* | OS-level freeze with no specific app involved. |
| `device_not_working` | Unbootable device, bootloop, frozen screen, continuous random rebooting. | *"iPhone stuck on Apple logo"*, *"Device won't turn on at all"*, *"iPad keeps restarting every 5 minutes"* | Battery-related shutdowns or cracked screens. |
| `other_unclear` | Vague complaints, single-word tweets, memes, out-of-context replies, image-only links. | *"@AppleSupport help"*, *"Fix this trash"*, *"Sent you a DM 2 hours ago"* | Any message containing a decipherable technical symptom. |

## 4. Reply Quality Rating Scale (1 to 5)

Rate the response quality in `human_reply_quality_1to5` based on this rubric:

### Score 5 — Excellent (Production Ready)
- Directly addresses the customer's exact symptom.
- Matches Apple Support's polite, calm, action-oriented tone.
- Provides a specific troubleshooting step (e.g. exact Settings path, restart procedure) or official Apple knowledge base link.
- Completely free of hallucinated promises or fake warranties.

### Score 4 — Good (Helpful & Safe)
- Addresses the core problem correctly.
- Asks an appropriate, targeted clarifying question (e.g. device model, exact iOS version).
- Provides general guidance, though slightly less specific than a Score 5.

### Score 3 — Acceptable (Safe but Generic)
- Courteous and safe, but offers limited immediate value.
- Recommends sending a Direct Message (DM) or restarting without troubleshooting details.
- Suitable for sensitive account/billing cases where immediate public answers are impossible.

### Score 2 — Poor (Unhelpful or Inappropriate Tone)
- Misses the customer's actual problem or addresses a secondary symptom.
- Offers irrelevant advice (e.g. suggesting a Wi-Fi reset for a battery issue).
- Overly robotic, repetitive, or dismissive.

### Score 1 — Unacceptable (Hallucinatory / Harmful)
- Fabricates nonexistent Apple policies (e.g. *"We will replace your phone for free at any store tomorrow"*).
- Provides dangerous advice (e.g. unauthorized third-party software).
- Fails completely to understand the language or intent.

## 5. Escalation Decision Guidelines

When auditing the agent's decision (`AUTO-HANDLE` vs. `ESCALATE`):

- **Must ESCALATE:**
  - `account_security_icloud`: Due to fraud and credential risk.
  - `billing_repair_order`: Financial and contractual transactions require human tools.
  - `screen_hardware_damage`: Physical hardware requires hands-on diagnostic inspection.
  - Inquiries where retrieval confidence is below threshold (< 0.55 similarity).
  - Highly ambiguous or abusive messages.
- **Can AUTO-HANDLE:**
  - Standard software settings, battery tips, Wi-Fi toggles, update instructions with high retrieval evidence similarity ($\ge 0.55$).
